Privacy policy

The short version: the calculators run entirely in your browser and send nothing anywhere. The only privacy question on this site is advertising, and you decide that yourself via the consent banner.

What the calculators collect

Nothing. Every calculation on this site — ingredient conversions, mayonnaise quantities — happens in JavaScript in your own browser. The numbers you type are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and are gone when you close the page. There are no accounts, no analytics scripts of our own, and no forms that submit anywhere.

What the site stores on your device

One value in your browser's local storage: your cookie-consent choice (accepted or declined), so we don't ask you again on every visit. It stays on your device, is not a tracking identifier, and is deleted if you clear your browser data.

Advertising and its cookies

This site plans to fund itself by showing ads through Google AdSense. Advertising services like AdSense use cookies and similar technologies to do two different things:

Personalised ads use cookies to recognise your browser across sites and show ads based on your interests and past browsing. Non-personalised ads are chosen from the context of the page alone, though they may still use cookies for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Google and its partners are third parties: when ads load, they receive your IP address and standard browser information, and their use of that data is governed by Google's policy on how it uses information from partner sites.

Your choice, and how to change it

No ad scripts load on this site until you make a choice in the consent banner. If you decline, ad scripts are not loaded at all. You can change your choice at any time via the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page, which reopens the banner.

Beyond this site, you can opt out of personalised advertising across Google services at Google Ads Settings, and learn about opting out of personalised advertising more broadly at youronlinechoices.eu (EU) or aboutads.info. Your browser can also block or delete cookies entirely; the calculators work fine without them.

Legal basis (EU/EEA visitors)

Under the GDPR and the ePrivacy rules, advertising cookies are used only with your consent, which the banner requests before anything loads. Declining has no effect on your use of the site. The consent choice itself is stored under legitimate interest, since remembering "no" is what makes "no" effective.

Changes and contact

If how this site works changes — for example when ads actually start running — this page will be updated to describe the practice as it is, not as boilerplate. Questions about this policy can be sent to the site operator via the contact address in the site's hosting registration.
