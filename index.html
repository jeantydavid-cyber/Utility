Why these tools?

Because the obvious shortcut gives a wrong answer. Generic cup converters quietly use water's density for everything, which is why online-converted baking recipes fail — flour, sugar and honey each need their own number. And mayonnaise recipes state quantities the wrong way around: the yolk count should follow from the oil, not the reverse, because it's the yolk's coating capacity that sets the limit.

Each tool explains its own arithmetic below the calculator, including what it deliberately leaves out. If a tool's honest answer is "buy a €15 kitchen scale and stop converting", it says so.
